Broken Patriot
A thief, betrayed by the nation he served. His wife lost in war. His patriotism corrupted into revenge.
Story

The Patriot's Fall

You were once a decorated soldier of your nation. When war threatened everything, you sacrificed yourself— not just your body, but your soul. Every mission, every life you took, was justified by the flag above.

But the war took a price you couldn't pay: your wife. During the chaos of an insurgent attack on the city, she was caught in the crossfire. The authorities called it "collateral damage." The nation mourned briefly, then moved on. They praised your heroism while ignoring the void in your heart.

Slowly, the truth unfolded. The war was built on lies. Leaders sacrificed civilians for territory. Soldiers like you were the tools of their ambition. Your patriotism was a shackle, and your wife's death was the cost of a lie you finally saw through.

Now, you are a thief. You steal what the nation holds most dear: its secrets, its resources, its pride. Every target is a wound reopened. Every job is an act of betrayal against those who betrayed you first.
